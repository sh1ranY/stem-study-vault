# Original signal construction brief

Original ELEC270-aligned demonstration, not an official handout.

## Shifted ramp and step conventions

r(t)=max(t,0); u(t)=0 for t<0 and 1 for t>=0. r(t-a) starts at zero at t=a. Evaluate the entire argument before applying the function.

## Plateau from a ramp difference

r(t)-r(t-2) is 0 for t<0, t for 0<=t<2, and 2 for t>=2. Equal slopes do not imply equal values. General plateau height m(b-a).

## Timed construction and endpoints

y(t)=r(t)-r(t-2)-2u(t-4). Final step removes height 2. y(4)=0 with u(0)=1. Energy: 8/3+8=32/3. Original adaptation, not official lecture text.
